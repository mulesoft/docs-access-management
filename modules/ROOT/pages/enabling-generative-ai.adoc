= Enabling or Disabling Generative AI for Anypoint Platform
:page-aliases: access-management::enabling-einstein.adoc, access-management::enabling-agentforce.adoc

Connect a trusted Salesforce organization and enable generative AI for your Anypoint Platform organization. After it's enabled, supported MuleSoft products can use it, and tracking and billing route through Salesforce. 

As an organization administrator, you can assign permission to the users in your organization so they can use generative AI with Anypoint Platform products that support it, including:

* Anypoint Code Builder
* Anypoint Exchange
* Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)

== Before You Begin

Before enabling generative AI in your Anypoint Platform organization, you must:
 
* Have administrative access to an Anypoint Platform organization that isn't a trial organization and isn't expired.
* Have the root Organization Administrator permission and role.
* Have administrative access to a Salesforce organization that https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ai.generative_ai_enable.htm&type=5[has Einstein Generative AI enabled]. 
* xref:access-management::trusted-salesforce-org.adoc[Establish a tenant relationship] between your Anypoint Platform organization and a trusted Salesforce organization. 

[[enable-einstein-salesforce]]
== Enable Generative AI for Anypoint Platform in the Salesforce Organization

When the Salesforce administrator enables generative AI for the connected Anypoint Platform organization, the connected Salesforce organization routes the requests. The connected Salesforce organization handles tracking and billing. 

. Log in to your Salesforce organization.
. In Salesforce Setup, enter `MuleSoft` in the *Quick Find* box, then select *MuleSoft > Anypoint Platform Setup*.
. In *Your connected Anypoint Platform Organization*, select *Complete Connection*.
. In *Connection to MuleSoft Anypoint Platform*, switch the setting that enables generative AI for Anypoint Platform to *Enabled*.
. Click *Close*.
+
After you enable it in the Salesforce organization, you see *Enabled in Salesforce* in Anypoint Platform for the connected Salesforce organization.

== Enable Generative AI for Your Anypoint Platform Organization

When generative AI is enabled in Anypoint Platform through a connected Salesforce organization, every supported feature in that organization can use it.

include::partial$include-nav-steps-org-admin.adoc[]

. Select *Salesforce*.
. When prompted to enable generative AI for Anypoint Platform, select *Accept*.
. Accept the terms and conditions.
+
The *Salesforce Orgs* section shows the orgs with xref:connecting-salesforce-orgs.adoc[established tenant relationships] with your Anypoint Platform organization. 
+
Each Salesforce organization shows the enabled and disabled Anypoint Platform capabilities.
. Select the Salesforce organization to use by default in your Anypoint Platform organization:
.. In *Capabilities managed in Salesforce*, select *Settings* for the capability that enhances the Anypoint Platform developer experience. 
.. Select the Salesforce organization, then select *Save*.


== Add the Required Permissions for Users

When generative AI is enabled for your Anypoint Platform organization, organization administrators are granted the required permission automatically. 

To grant other users access, assign the required permissions manually.

include::partial$include-nav-steps-org-admin.adoc[]

. xref:configure-teams.adoc[Create a team] for the users who need access to generative AI.
. Select the *Permissions* tab.
. In *Permissions*, select *Add permissions*.
. Select the *Anypoint Code Builder > Mule Developer Generative AI User* permission and select *Next*.
. Select the business groups to apply the permissions to. 
+
Select which environments to apply the permission to if it's enabled for the permission. 
. Select *Next*.
. Review the permissions and the business groups and environments they apply to, and then select *Add Permissions*.

== Disable Generative AI

When you disable generative AI for your Anypoint Platform organization, none of the users in your organization can use it in any of the features where it's enabled.

include::partial$include-nav-steps-org-admin.adoc[]

. Select *Salesforce*.
. In the *Capabilities managed in Salesforce* section, switch the capability that enhances the Anypoint Platform developer experience to *Disabled*.
