= Accessing MuleSoft Anypoint Platform from Your Salesforce Home Org

After you are assigned the *Anypoint User* or *Anypoint Admin* permission:  

. Log in to your Salesforce Home org.
+
After you log in, you see the *Anypoint Home* tab. 
. Click *Continue to Anypoint Platform*.
+
You see the Anypoint Platform landing page.
. Optionally, select *Skip this page when opening Anypoint*. 
If you select this option, the next time you log in, you go directly to the Anypoint Platform landing page, rather than the Salesforce Home org page. 
. To return to the Salesforce Home org, click the Salesforce logo. 
+
