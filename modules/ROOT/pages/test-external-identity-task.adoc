Verify that your external identity provider (IdP) integration for single sign-on (SSO) is functioning correctly. Learn how to obtain your organization's specific sign-in URL from the Access Management settings. Test the complete login flow by navigating to this URL, authenticating with a test user via your IdP, and confirming successful redirection back to Anypoint Platform.

include::partial$include-nav-organizations.adoc[]

. Click the *Settings* tab. 

. Copy your organization name from the Domain Name field.
+
. Sign out of your Organization Administrator account in Anypoint Platform.
. Navigate to your organization’s signin URL.
+
You are redirected to your identity provider.
+
. Sign in to your identity provider with a test user managed by your identity provider.
+
After authenticating, you are redirected back to Anypoint Platform as an authenticated user.
